How it works

Pick a mode. Exam simulation runs the real length, the real timer, and the real area weighting, graded at the end. Study mode reveals the answer as you go. Menu drills put you in a drawn console screen and ask where you'd click. Weak spots rebuilds a set from exactly what you've been getting wrong.

Answer, then read. The explanation names what the question was testing, states the answer plainly, and then goes through the options you didn't choose. That last part is the point — most exam mistakes are two features that sound alike, and you only separate them by learning what the other one does.

The app remembers. Accuracy per topic, every attempt, and which individual questions still catch you. It'll tell you your three weakest topics and build a set from them on request.

What this isn't

Not real exam questions

Nothing here is copied from an actual exam. These are original questions written from the publicly published skills outlines and vendor documentation. Sites that repost leaked exam content put your certification at risk — using them breaks the agreement you sign before sitting an exam, and results get revoked over it. That's why this took longer to build and why it teaches better.

Not affiliated with any vendor

acuExam is independent. Exam codes and product names belong to their owners and are used only to say which exam a pack covers. Console screens in the menu drills are drawn recreations built from public documentation, not screenshots.

Not a substitute for the official material

Practice questions find your gaps; they don't fill them. Every pack links to the vendor's own study guide and free learning path — read those, then come back and see what still catches you.
